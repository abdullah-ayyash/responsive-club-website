A friendly place to play the game of kings

Chess enthusiasts of all ages and skill levels come together at Richmond Chess Club. We're dedicated to fostering a love of the game in our community, and everyone who shares that passion is welcome to join us.

Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned player, you'll find a friendly environment to improve your skills, meet new people and enjoy some friendly competition.

We believe chess brings people together, so we work to keep the club a safe, inclusive space where every member feels respected.
